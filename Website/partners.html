How the partnership works

You introduce a practice

An email is enough. There is no portal to learn, no referral code to remember and no minimum number. Introduce one client or twenty.

They try it free for a month

Connected to their Dentally in minutes, with no card. The first month includes a data health check on their own records — their overdue recalls, their lapsed patients. If it isn't useful they walk away having spent nothing, and so have you.

You earn 20%, for as long as they stay

Of everything they pay us, every month — not a one-off finder's fee. Ten practices introduced is a recurring five-figure annual income from work you did once.

We self-bill you monthly

We raise the invoice on your behalf and pay it by bank transfer, so there is nothing for you to raise and nothing to chase. It needs a short written self-billing agreement and your VAT number first — we'll send both.

Before you put your name to it

Introducing a supplier to a client puts your relationship on the line, not just ours. We hold patient data, so you should expect to see how — before you recommend us to anyone.

Written down, not asserted

Data protection impact assessment, data processing agreement, security overview, sub-processor register. Read them before you introduce anyone.

Only what it needs

No clinical notes, no special-category data. Once a patient is made inactive in Dentally, their identifying details are removed from the platform automatically.

UK-hosted, isolated per practice

Azure UK South. One practice's data is unreachable from another's, enforced by the database rather than by the application.

We will talk to your client first

Happy to take a call with a practice manager before anything is connected, so the questions land with us rather than with you.

Start with one client.

Tell us who they are, or just ask us the awkward questions first — we would rather answer them now than after you have made the introduction.
