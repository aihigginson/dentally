Create your access token in Dentally
  1. In Dentally, open Settings → Personal Access Tokens and choose New personal access token.
  2. Tick every “read” permission: user:read, appointment:read, financials:read, correspondence:read, patient:read, practice:read and treatments.
  3. Click Save, copy the token it shows you, and paste it below.
