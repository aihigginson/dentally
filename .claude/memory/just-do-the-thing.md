When asked to do something, **do it**. Do not precede it with option lists, risk inventories or
paragraphs explaining why the obvious route is imperfect.

Triggered while publishing a notebook change and running an ingest. The user: *"I do feel you are
making this much more problematic than it should be."* Earlier in the same session: *"stop keep
reminding me about the test gate."*

**Why:** they are a solo builder who knows their own system. Ceremony reads as hesitancy and costs
them time. A constraint worth mentioning is worth one sentence, not three paragraphs and a menu.

**How to apply:** if a real blocker means the task cannot be done as asked, say so in a line and
offer the single best alternative — not two or three weighed against each other. Otherwise act,
then report what happened. Keep risk notes to the ones that would change their decision. See
[[dont-re-raise-settled-decisions]].
