A metric on the Demonstration Practice reading badly is **not automatically a generator fault**. I
flagged Cancel % 37.2% (target 31%) and Book Before You Leave 54.1% (target 65%) as rates to fix.
The owner's answer: leave them, they are the story.

- **Cancellations are routinely far higher than owners believe.** A demo showing 37% opens exactly
  the conversation the product exists to start, and the rebook problem behind it is real.
- **Low Book Before You Leave has a known, cheap fix** — the dentist walks the patient to the front
  desk and hands over out loud: "Sharon will see to you and book you in for six months." A demo
  that shows the number low is demonstrating an actionable finding, not a data error.
- **Rebooked at 98.3% is unusually GOOD**, and that is the point too: it "shows what you are doing
  right as well as wrong". A board that is all red is as useless a pitch as one that is all green.

**Why:** the demo sells by being recognisable. A practice owner who sees only green learns nothing
and believes none of it; a mix is what makes them ask about their own numbers.

**How to apply:** before proposing a generator change to make a demo figure "look right", ask
whether the figure is implausible or merely unflattering. Structural impossibilities still need
fixing -- see [[calibrate-the-demo-tenant-against-live]] and the decay that made Open (No Appt) 92%
of open courses -- but a plausible bad number is finished work. The owner tunes the targets; the
ratios only have to be viable.
