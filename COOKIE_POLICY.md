# Cookie Policy — Analytically

**Last updated: 2026-06-30.**

This policy explains the cookies used on the Analytically marketing website
(`analytically.info`) and how you can control them. It covers this public website only —
the Analytically application (`app.analytically.info`) is a separate, authenticated
service and does not use these cookies.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for a site
to work; others help the operator understand how the site is used.

## The cookies we use

We keep this to a minimum. This website uses **Google Analytics 4** to understand, in
aggregate, how visitors use the site — for example which pages are viewed and roughly
where traffic comes from — so we can improve it. We do **not** use cookies for
advertising, profiling or cross-site tracking.

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Expiry |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Distinguishes one visitor from another | ~2 years |
| `_ga_VZ89XSHWQZ` | Google Analytics | Keeps session state for our specific analytics property | ~2 years |

These are first-party cookies. The usage data they generate is processed by **Google** as
our analytics provider; Google Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses. Any
transfer of data outside the UK takes place under Google's own data-transfer safeguards.
See our [Sub-processor Register](/doc.html?doc=SUB_PROCESSOR_REGISTER) for the third
parties we rely on.

The enquiry form does not set cookies.

## No patient or practice data

These cookies relate only to your use of this public website. They do **not** involve any
dental practice or patient data, which is held in our secured application — see the
[Security Overview](/doc.html?doc=SECURITY_OVERVIEW).

## Your choices

Analytics cookies are optional and are set **only if you accept them**. When you first
visit, a banner asks for your consent — **no analytics cookies are set, and Google
Analytics is not loaded, unless you choose "Accept"**. You can change your decision at
any time using the **"Cookie settings"** link in the page footer.

You can also:

- **Adjust your browser settings** to block or delete cookies at any time.
- Install Google's [Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- Use your browser's private / incognito mode.

Declining analytics cookies will not affect your ability to use the website.
